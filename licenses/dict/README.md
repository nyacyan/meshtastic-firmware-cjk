# Dictionary licences

The Bopomofo input method embeds a dictionary generated from third-party corpora.
No corpus file is shipped with this firmware; only the derived trie and its
candidate blob are embedded. The full terms for every source are kept here.

## src/graphics/zhuyin_single_static.h, src/graphics/zhuyin_trie_static.h

Generated by `tools/zhuyin_trie_generator.py`.

| Source | Role | Terms | File |
|--------|------|-------|------|
| [openvanilla/McBopomofo](https://github.com/openvanilla/McBopomofo) - `BPMFMappings.txt`, `BPMFBase.txt`, `phrase.occ` | Every reading and every word entry, and the corpus frequencies that decide candidate order | MIT | `LICENSE-mcbopomofo.txt` |
| libtabe - `tsi.src` | Upstream of McBopomofo's multi-character phrases | BSD 3-Clause | `LICENSE-libtabe.txt` |
| [chewing/libchewing-data](https://github.com/chewing/libchewing-data) - `dict/chewing/tsi.csv` | Real-word whitelist for the single-character build | LGPL-2.1-or-later | `LICENSE-libchewing-lgpl-2.1.txt` |

McBopomofo's own data notes record that its multi-character phrases were
"originally simplified from tsi.src of libtabe (BSD Licensed) with
modifications", so the phrase entries carry libtabe's terms as well. libtabe's
`COPYING` holds two BSD 3-Clause grants, one from the TaBE Project and
Pai-Hsiang Hsiao, one from the Computer Systems and Communication Lab at the
Institute of Information Science, Academia Sinica; both are reproduced in
`LICENSE-libtabe.txt`. The single-character readings in `BPMFBase.txt` and the
frequencies in `phrase.occ` carry no upstream attribution of their own.

`tsi.csv` is used as a filter, not as a source of entries: it decides which of
McBopomofo's multi-character words are real words and which are segmentation
debris to drop. No entry is copied from it, and it does not reach the firmware
in any form. It is applied to the single-character dictionary only; the
60000-word build is generated from McBopomofo alone.
